3. Unresolved Limitations:
--------------------------
  - FVM spatial convergence order on skewed tetrahedral meshes is constrained to ~0.87 (first-order)
    because cell-centered face normal gradients omit secondary cross-diffusion corrections.
  - Non-monotonic spatial convergence of T_max across tetrahedral mesh refinement levels under
    concentrated boundary heat flux (Neumann source patch), rendering multi-grid Richardson
    extrapolation mathematically invalid for peak temperatures.
  - Voxel staircase boundary representation in FDM introduces a persistent ~0.41 °C cold bias
    and requires 41× more degrees of freedom (487k vs 11.8k) to resolve tapered fin profiles.
