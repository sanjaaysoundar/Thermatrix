1. Matrix Symmetry & Positive-Definiteness Handling:
----------------------------------------------------
Preconditioned Conjugate Gradient (PCG) requires a symmetric positive-definite (SPD) matrix.
  - Symmetry: The discrete FVM face diffusion conductances satisfy:
        A_PN = -k * A_f / d_PN = A_NP
    yielding exact matrix symmetry: ||A - A^T||_F / ||A||_F = 0.00 across all grid resolutions.
  - Positive Definiteness: Positive diagonal entries alone (A_PP > 0) DO NOT mathematically
    guarantee positive definiteness. Instead, positive definiteness is strictly established via
    the Gershgorin circle theorem combined with Robin boundary physics:
      * Interior rows are weakly diagonally dominant: sum_{N in nb(P)} |A_PN| = A_PP.
      * Robin convective boundary faces add strictly positive conductance:
            A_PP = sum_{N} |A_PN| + sum_{b} h * A_b
        where h * A_b > 0.
      * Because the unstructured tetrahedral mesh is a connected graph (irreducible) and
        contains strictly diagonally dominant boundary rows with positive diagonals, A is
        irreducibly diagonally dominant.
      * By the Taussky theorem, all eigenvalues lambda_i > 0, rigorously guaranteeing that A
        is strictly positive definite and PCG-IC(0) is unconditionally breakdown-free without
        any diagonal clamping or ad-hoc regularisation.

  directly into the single adjacent control volume cell (b_P += q*A).
