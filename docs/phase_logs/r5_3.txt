1. Temperature Average Definitions & Discretisation Principles:
----------------------------------------------------------------
In 3D thermal simulations on non-uniform unstructured grids (e.g., P1 tetrahedral meshes
used by FEM and FVM), element/dual-cell volumes vary by orders of magnitude across the domain:
  - FEM/FVM tetrahedral volumes range from 5.23e-10 m^3 (dense refinement near source patch)
    to 3.03e-9 m^3 (coarser regions in base and fin tips), an element volume ratio of 5.80x.
  - A raw arithmetic mean over nodes or cell centroids (T_avg_raw = sum(T_i) / N) gives equal
    weight to small elements in high-density clusters and large elements in low-density zones,
    imparting an artificial bias toward the local temperature of the densely refined region.
  - In contrast, the rigorous continuum volume-weighted average temperature is defined as:
        T_avg_vol = (1 / V_total) * integral_Omega T(x, y, z) dV
    Discretised as:
        FEM: sum_e (V_e * sum_{i in e} T_i / 4) / sum_e V_e
        FVM: sum_P (V_P * T_P) / sum_P V_P
        FDM: sum_{ijk in solid} (h^3 * T_{ijk}) / sum_{ijk in solid} h^3  [equivalent to raw due to uniform h]
