r4-6: Trilinear FDM & Green-Gauss FVM Probe Interpolation
============================================================

1. Implementation:
- FDM Probe Sampling (`sample_fdm`):
  Replaced nearest-cell lookup with true 8-point trilinear interpolation in
  cell-centered coordinates. For probe locations near staircase solid boundaries,
  non-solid cells in the 8-corner stencil are substituted by the nearest solid cell
  among the corners, preventing extrapolation outside the domain while preserving
  linear convergence in solid interiors.
- FVM Probe Sampling (`sample_fvm`):
  Replaced piecewise-constant cell value with cell value plus Green-Gauss gradient
  correction: T(pt) ≈ T_P + ∇T_P · (pt - x_P), where ∇T_P = (1/V_P) ∑_f T_face * S_f.
  Face values are reconstructed by averaging adjacent cell centroids.
- Sampling methods explicitly recorded in `results/validation_results.csv`:
  * FEM: barycentric interpolation over containing tetrahedron
  * FDM: trilinear interpolation (nearest solid near staircase)
  * FVM: cell value plus Green-Gauss gradient correction
