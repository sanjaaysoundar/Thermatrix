2. Root Cause Analysis & Invalidation:
--------------------------------------
  - For the 9-Fin T_max grid series across Coarse, Medium, and Fine levels, the localized
    Neumann source patch creates steep thermal gradients such that convergence is non-monotonic
    or |e32| < |e21| (observed order ratio < 1.0, leading to a negative apparent order).
  - The legacy lambda routine caught this via `if (p < 0.5 || p > 4.0) p = p_def;` and forcibly
    clamped the convergence rate to an arbitrary default `p = 2.0`.
  - Evaluating Richardson extrapolation with a clamped p=2.0 on non-monotonic data generated
    synthetic numbers: 61.0295 °C (FEM), 60.3265 °C (FDM), 60.5567 °C (FVM).
  - Under ASME V&V 20 and Roache (1998) GCI guidelines, Richardson extrapolation is mathematically
    invalid unless asymptotic monotonic convergence (0 < e21/e32 < 1) is achieved naturally
    without clamping, artificial exponent substitution, or sign concealment.
  - Conclusion: 61.0295, 60.3265, and 60.5567 are non-physical numerical artifacts caused by
    unphysical order clamping. They are marked unverified and completely removed from all benchmark
    tables, consensus evaluations, and conclusions. Consensus T_max is correctly reported as
    UNAVAILABLE / NaN.
